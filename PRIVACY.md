# Privacy policy

**One Clipboard History — Armateur Labs**

Last updated: 2026-11-04

One Clipboard History keeps clipboard history **on your Mac**. Copied data,
available formats and metadata, file references, and the source application name
are used to capture, display, and restore your copies. They are not transmitted
to Armateur Labs or third parties. The app includes no advertising, analytics,
tracking, account, or network synchronization.

### Permissions and control

Clipboard access enables capture. Event-posting permission enables pasting into
the previous application when you request it. If that permission is denied, you
can copy an entry and paste it manually with ⌘V. Permissions can be revoked in
System Settings. Launch at login is optional and off by default. Quit the app
from its menu to stop capture.

### Retention and deletion

History is held in memory by default and is discarded when the app exits.
Enabling persistence stores copies and file references locally in Application
Support (inside the app container for the sandboxed variant). The app does not
encrypt this storage; your macOS account protections and FileVault, if enabled,
apply.

History length and data budget are configurable. Older entries are removed when
these limits are exceeded. “Clear History” deletes retained copies. Disabling
persistence deletes the local history storage while keeping the current session
in memory. Referenced original files are never deleted by these actions. Your
own macOS backups, including Time Machine, may retain previous versions.

The clipboard can contain sensitive information. The app cannot identify every
secret or password; you control what you copy and keep in history.
